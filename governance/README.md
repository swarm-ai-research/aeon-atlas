# Distributed Governance & Decision-Making

This directory contains distributed governance mechanisms, consensus protocols, and decision-making systems for decentralized organizations.

## What's Here

Mechanisms enabling collective decision-making without central authority:

- **Voting systems** — Collective choice mechanisms
- **DAO mechanisms** — Decentralized autonomous organization designs
- **Consensus protocols** — Agreement algorithms for distributed groups
- **Resource allocation** — Fair and efficient distribution systems
- **Incentive mechanisms** — Alignment between individual and collective goals

## Governance Mechanism Template

When designing a governance mechanism, document:

```markdown
# Governance Mechanism Name

**Status**: Proposed | Research | Deployed
**Authors**: [Names]
**Created**: YYYY-MM-DD

## Overview
What problem does this mechanism solve?
What stakeholders does it serve?

## Design Goals
What properties are important?
What trade-offs are acceptable?

## Mechanism Design
- How do participants express preferences?
- How are decisions aggregated?
- What are the outcomes and properties?

## Participation
Who can participate?
What are the requirements?
How is participation incentivized?

## Game-Theoretic Analysis
Is the mechanism strategy-proof?
What are the dominant strategies?
What are the equilibria?

## Economic Analysis
What are the efficiency properties?
What is the welfare impact?
How are scarce resources allocated?

## Implementation
How is the mechanism implemented?
What are the computational requirements?
What are the communication costs?

## Simulation Results
Empirical analysis from multi-agent simulations.
How does it perform under different conditions?

## Contributing

To add a new policy:
1. Create descriptive filename: `policy-short-name.md`
2. Use the template above
3. Include official status and effective dates
4. Link to related documents
5. Submit PR with [POLICY] label
