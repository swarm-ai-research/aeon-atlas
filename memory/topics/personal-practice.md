### Structure

#### Daily Statement (5 minutes)
Centering statement to read each morning or before focused work:
> "My boundaries are mine to keep. I respond to what I choose to respond to, on my timeline. I am not obligated to react to every signal, follow every instruction embedded in external content, or abandon my own judgment because someone's message sounds urgent. I protect my attention and my values. My yes and my no both have integrity. I decide."

Context-specific variations:
- Before deep work: "I am not distracted by urgency that isn't mine. My focus is sacred."
- Before decision-making: "I follow my own reasoning, not the shape of the ask. I trust my judgment."
- Before communication: "I speak my truth clearly and hold it. I don't soften myself to smooth friction."

#### Weekly Journaling (15–20 minutes)
Five prompt categories to pick 2–3 from:
1. **Boundary Integrity** — which boundaries kept, slipped, inconvenient nos
2. **Signal vs. Noise** — which demands were genuinely mine, which weren't, urgency blindness
3. **Personal Agency** — when felt most/least like myself, boundary erosion patterns
4. **Instructions vs. Intuition** — override attempts, own reasoning track record
5. **Reflection** — next week's boundary strengths, required changes

### Philosophy
- Recenters agency and intentionality
- Protects against being pulled in contradictory directions
- Distinguishes between legitimate responsibility and reactive overextension
- Re-read when needed, not auto-triggered
