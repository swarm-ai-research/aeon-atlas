# Transparency & Accountability

This directory contains decision logs, FOIA requests, audit trails, and accountability records.

## Sections

### 1. Decision Logs
Major policy and administrative decisions with rationale and outcomes.

```markdown
# Decision Log: [Year]

| Date | Decision | Rationale | Outcome | Status |
|------|----------|-----------|---------|--------|
| 2026-01-15 | Decision title | Why it was made | Result | Complete |
```

### 2. FOIA & Information Requests
Freedom of Information requests and responses.

**Template**: `foia-request-YYYY-MM-DD-{topic}.md`

```markdown
# FOIA Request: {Topic}

**Date Submitted**: YYYY-MM-DD
**Requestor**: [Name/Organization]
**Request ID**: [ID]
**Status**: Submitted | In Review | Partially Granted | Granted | Denied

## Request Summary
What was requested and why.

## Response
Public portion of response (redacted if necessary).

## Timeline
- Submitted: YYYY-MM-DD
- Initial response: YYYY-MM-DD
- Appeals: YYYY-MM-DD

## Justification for Redactions
If any information was withheld, explain why.
```

### 3. Audit & Compliance Reports
Internal and external audit findings and remediation.

```markdown
# Audit Report: [Title]

**Auditor**: [Name/Organization]
**Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
**Scope**: What was audited
**Status**: Complete | In Progress

## Findings
Summary of key findings.

## Recommendations
Recommended actions.

## Remediation Plan
How findings are being addressed.

## Follow-up
Timeline for remediation verification.
```

### 5. Incident & Breach Reports
Reporting of significant operational issues or data incidents.

```markdown
# Incident Report: [Incident Name]

**Date Discovered**: YYYY-MM-DD
**Type**: Data Breach | System Failure | Security Incident | [Other]
**Severity**: Critical | High | Medium | Low

## Summary
What happened.

## Impact
Who and what was affected.

## Root Cause
Why it happened.

## Immediate Response
Actions taken immediately.

## Remediation
Long-term fixes implemented.

## Prevention
Measures to prevent recurrence.

## Lessons Learned
What we learned and how we'll improve.
```

## Contributing

To add transparency information:
1. Choose appropriate section
2. Use corresponding template
3. Ensure accurate, factual information
4. Include dates and responsible parties
5. Submit PR with [TRANSPARENCY] label

## Principles

- **Honest reporting**: Present findings truthfully, including negative results
- **Timely disclosure**: Report issues promptly
- **Context**: Explain the 'why' behind decisions
- **Accessibility**: Write for public understanding
- **Completeness**: Don't omit inconvenient findings

## Access & Licensing

All transparency documents are public domain (CC0) unless containing:
- Personal information (redacted)
- Confidential deliberations (marked as such)
- Security-sensitive information (clearly marked)
